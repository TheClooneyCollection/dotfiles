# catch-up

A brief of what changed since your last message, in a fixed shape that brings the current state and any decision into view.

**Why:** I come back from a break or a flood of agent notices and ask "catch me up" or "cmu". I want only what changed since my last message, concise and in a fixed shape: what landed, what is pending or blocked, and a decision only if one is needed. Long recaps bury the decision and repeat messages I can already read.

**Use it:** say "catch me up", "cmu", "since my last message", "what happened", 发生了什么 or 进展.

## What it does

- Covers only what changed since your last message.
- Gives what landed and the state now, then pending or blocked work, then a decision question with options when needed.
- Omits empty parts and keeps each part to one or two sentences.
- Leaves out messages you can already read, tool narration, preambles and closings.
- Keeps immediate questions about what the agent is doing or why it is slow separate from a catch-up.
