# Agents that need the user are pinned at the top of the list

**Decision.** Sub agents waiting for permission or marked "needs you" are pinned in a section at the top of the agent list, whichever window they belong to and in both scopes, so nothing waiting on the user is hidden by "this window". Each shows its window and project. They're sorted by how long they've been waiting (longest first), and they're listed only there, not again in their project's section.

**Why.** Being filtered out of view is worst for exactly the agents that are blocked on the user.
